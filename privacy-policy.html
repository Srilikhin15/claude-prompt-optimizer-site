Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy applies to Claude Prompt Optimizer, an instruction-based plugin that helps users improve prompts for Claude while using ChatGPT.

The plugin is maintained by Sri Likhin. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

2. How the plugin works

The current plugin uses instructions within ChatGPT to optimize prompt text. It does not operate a separate plugin backend or database, connect to the Claude API, or use a Claude tokenizer to calculate exact token counts.

When you use the plugin, your messages and the generated responses are processed as part of the ChatGPT service. OpenAI's own privacy policy and applicable data controls govern that processing. The plugin publisher does not receive those messages through a separate, publisher-run plugin server.

3. Information that may be processed

  • Prompt content: Text you enter and the resulting output may be processed by ChatGPT to provide the requested optimization.
  • Support messages: If you contact the publisher, your email address, name if provided, message, and any information you choose to include may be received.
  • Website technical information: The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as request and security logs, under its own policies.

4. Purposes of processing

Information may be used to:

  • Provide prompt optimization through ChatGPT.
  • Respond to support requests and resolve reported issues.
  • Host and protect the public information website.
  • Meet applicable legal or security obligations.

5. Sharing and service providers

The plugin does not independently send your prompts to Anthropic or another external AI API. You may choose to copy an optimized prompt into Claude yourself.

OpenAI processes ChatGPT conversations under its own policies. GitHub provides website hosting. If you email the publisher, your email service provider will also process that communication. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

6. Data retention

Support emails and related correspondence are retained for up to 12 months after the support request is resolved, so that follow-up questions and recurring issues can be handled. After this period, the correspondence is deleted unless it must be retained for a legitimate legal, security, or dispute-resolution purpose. Requests for earlier deletion will be considered where feasible and where retention is not legally required.

The plugin publisher does not independently store prompt content or generated responses on a publisher-operated plugin server. OpenAI's data retention practices and the data controls available through ChatGPT apply to ChatGPT conversations. Website hosting records are handled by GitHub under its own policies.

7. Your choices and controls

  • Avoid submitting passwords, API keys, confidential business information, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary and appropriate.
  • Use the data controls provided by ChatGPT to manage your conversations and related data.
  • Contact the publisher to request deletion of support correspondence held by the publisher. Legal obligations may limit deletion in some circumstances.

The publisher cannot control data that OpenAI or GitHub processes or retains under its own policies.
